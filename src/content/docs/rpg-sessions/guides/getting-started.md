Start here if you need to create an RPG Sessions account, find your games and characters, or connect Discord before joining a table.

## Creating an Account

Head to [rpgsessions.com](https://rpgsessions.com) and click **Sign Up**. You'll need to provide a username, email address, and password. Once you've created your account and logged in, you'll land on your dashboard.

After your account is set up, you can link your Discord account from your profile settings to enable [Discord bot](/docs/discord-bot) features.

## The Dashboard

The dashboard shows the games and characters connected to your account.

### My Games

This section lists every game table you created or joined. Each card shows the game name, system, and player count. Select a game to open its table.

If the list is empty, you can [create a game](/docs/rpg-sessions/guides/creating-a-game) or join one with a code from your GM.

### My Characters

Your character library is available outside any individual game. You can open and manage a sheet here, then add that character to a game table when you need it.

### Navigation

The top navigation bar gives you quick access to:

- **Games** - Your game tables
- **Characters** - Your character list
- **Account** - Profile settings, linked accounts, and subscription info

## Linking Your Discord Account

If you signed up with email (or want to verify your Discord is connected), you can link your Discord account from your profile settings.

1. Go to your **Account** settings
2. Find the Discord section
3. Click **Link Discord** and authorize the connection

Once linked, the [RPG Sessions Discord Bot](/docs/discord-bot) can access your characters and game data directly from Discord. This lets you roll dice, view sheets, and sync game events without switching to the browser.

:::tip
You don't need to link Discord to use the web platform. It's only required if you want to use the Discord bot features.
:::

## What's Next

Choose the next guide based on what you need to do:

- **Starting a campaign?** [Create a game table](/docs/rpg-sessions/guides/creating-a-game) and invite your players
- **Joining someone else's game?** Ask your GM for the game code or invite link
- **Want to build a character?** Check out the [character sheets overview](/docs/rpg-sessions/characters) to learn what's available
- **Need the Discord bot?** Follow the [Discord bot setup guide](/docs/discord-bot) to get it running in your server

Need help? Join the [community Discord](https://discord.gg/DfEkRzUbjk) and ask in the support channels.
