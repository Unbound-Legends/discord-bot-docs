RPG Sessions is the virtual tabletop built specifically for **Star Wars RPG** (Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny) and **Genesys**. It handles the things that make narrative dice games awkward to play online: character sheets with all the crunch baked in, automatic dice symbol calculation, and a game table that keeps your whole group on the same page.

Whether you're running a weekly campaign or a one-shot with friends scattered across time zones, RPG Sessions gives you a shared space to manage characters, roll dice, track initiative, and run encounters without juggling spreadsheets and manual math.

## What You Can Do

### Game Tables

Create a game table for your campaign and invite your players. Everyone joins the same space where they can add characters, roll dice in a shared chat, and track initiative together. GMs get additional controls for managing NPCs, encounters, and game settings.

[Learn more about game tables](/docs/rpg-sessions/game-table)

### Character Sheets

Full digital character sheets for Star Wars RPG and Genesys. Characteristics, skills, talents, equipment, weapons, force powers, injuries, and everything else your system tracks. Sheets update in real-time, so your GM always sees your current wound count without asking.

[Learn more about character sheets](/docs/rpg-sessions/characters)

### Dice Rolling

Roll the full set of narrative dice (Boost, Setback, Ability, Difficulty, Proficiency, Challenge, and Force dice) with automatic symbol calculation. Results show Success, Failure, Advantage, Threat, Triumph, Despair, and Force pips without anyone counting symbols manually.

### Vehicle Sheets

Dedicated vehicle sheets for starships, speeders, walkers, and other craft. Track hull trauma, system strain, weapons, attachments, and crew positions with the same detail as character sheets.

### Character Imports

Already have a character built in OggDude's Character Generator or SWCharGen Emporium? Import it directly into RPG Sessions instead of rebuilding from scratch.

### Discord Integration

The [RPG Sessions Discord Bot](/docs/discord-bot) connects your game to Discord. Roll dice, view character sheets, sync initiative, and get session notifications without leaving your server.

### Interactive Maps

[Sessions Maps](/docs/maps) adds tactical gameplay to your sessions with real-time battle maps, character tokens linked to your sheets, fog of war, and grid overlays. Maps is available to Patreon supporters.
